# Resumoo 📄

**Build your next opportunity.**

Resumoo is a resume-building platform designed to help students, graduates, interns, and job seekers create and improve their resumes with a simpler, more accessible, and affordable approach.

The goal is to make professional resume creation easier, helping users present their skills, experience, and achievements with confidence.

## 🎯 The Problem

Creating a professional resume can be challenging, especially for students and people entering the job market. Professional resume services can be expensive, while creating a strong resume independently can take time and effort.

## 💡 The Solution

Resumoo aims to simplify the resume-building process by making professional resume creation more accessible, efficient, and affordable.

## ✨ Key Objectives

- Make resume creation easier for students and job seekers.
- Help users present their qualifications clearly and professionally.
- Reduce the time and effort required to build a resume.
- Make career preparation more accessible and affordable.

## 👥 Who Is It For?

- University and high school students
- Recent graduates
- Internship applicants
- Job seekers
- Anyone looking to improve their resume
